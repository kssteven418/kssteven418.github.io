About

I am an Assistant Professor at KAIST AI, where I lead the Scalable and Efficient AI Lab (SEAL). If you are interested in joining, please read this note.

Previously, I built Grok at xAI, focusing on inference and RL infrastructure. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UC Berkeley, advised by Prof. Kurt Keutzer. During my Ph.D., I was selected as a MLCommons ML and Systems Rising Star and was a finalist for the NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship in 2024. I was also a founding engineer at Narada AI during my Ph.D.
